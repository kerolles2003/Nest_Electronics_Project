# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Students, makers, robotics builders, and electronics buyers in Egypt who need reliable Arduino, ESP32, sensors, modules, tools, and project parts. They may be shopping for a graduation project, restocking a lab bench, or comparing components for an engineering build. They value fast local availability, trustworthy stock, clear product scanning, and practical technical support.

## Product Purpose

Nest Electronics is a customer-facing electronics marketplace for finding and buying maker and embedded-systems components with confidence. The homepage should create a clear shopping path, make search dominant, foreground local availability and trust signals, and help users quickly move from project intent to relevant products or categories.

## Brand Personality

Clean, practical, trustworthy, technical, and modern. The brand should feel like a student/maker marketplace blended with premium electronics retail: approachable enough for project builders, precise enough for engineers, and polished enough to feel reliable.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI ecommerce layouts, glassmorphism, over-animated UI, template-style gradients, floating action pills, excessive shadows, rounded playful cards, and developer-style decoration that does not help buyers make decisions.

## Design Principles

1. Search is the primary shopping path.
2. Trust should be concrete: local dispatch, genuine components, cash on delivery, and engineer support.
3. Product scanning beats decoration.
4. Technical tone should come from useful specificity, not styling gimmicks.
5. Use a flat, sharp, premium retail system with minimal motion and restrained emphasis.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-accessible product browsing. Keep critical actions visible on touch devices, avoid hover-only functionality for shopping decisions, respect reduced motion, and design for long product names, mobile interruptions, and mixed technical experience levels.
